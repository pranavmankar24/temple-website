Sacred Heritage

A living Shiva temple in the heart of Akola

|| शिवो भूत्वा शिवं यजेत् ||

Shri Rajrajeshwar Temple stands as the revered Gram Devta of Akola, carrying a magnificent spiritual legacy of nearly 500 to 700 years. The temple houses a self-manifested Shivlinga, drawing devotees who come for darshan, abhishek and the quiet strength of Lord Shiva's blessings.
