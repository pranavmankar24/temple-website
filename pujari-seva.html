How It Works

Simple direct contact, no booking system

This page is designed like a respectful directory. Devotees can understand which pujari may help with their puja need and contact them directly.

Before contacting

  • Confirm puja type, date and preferred time.
  • Ask what samagri or offerings are needed.
  • Discuss dakshina respectfully and directly.
  • Follow temple rules for photography and timings.
